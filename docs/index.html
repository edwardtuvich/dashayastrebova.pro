Common questions

Who owns the images and music?

Before work starts, we agree in writing on usage: channels, territories and term. The final assets are licensed to you on those terms. I do not use real people's likenesses, logos or trademarks without permission.

Do you disclose AI use?

Yes. Every project comes with a written note on where and how AI was used, so your legal team knows exactly what it is working with.

When is AI the wrong choice?

When something has to be exact, like a product close-up. Then I shoot the real product and build the world around it. I'm a live-action director first, so mixing a real shoot with AI comes naturally.

Can we reuse our AI models next season?

Yes. In YAFIT by Perel the same two models carry the whole campaign. You get their references with the files.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. In the first week you get a schedule with fixed review points, so you always know what comes next.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide. Invoicing in USD or EUR.

Can we work across time zones?

Yes. We set one regular call at a time that suits you, and everything else runs on shared boards and written feedback.
