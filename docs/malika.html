Films/MALIKA

A film portrait

MALIKA

A film portrait of the French photographer Malika Mokadem, my muse. Not a biography but a portrait of an artist's inner world.

Black and white · in English · in post-production

Teaser. Edit: Dan Jamkinsun. Song: “Anabasis”

The film

The inner world of an artist

Malika mixes symbols of different faiths with her own memories. I wanted to film not her biography but this inner world: her symbols, her home, her travels, the many women inside her.

For me it is the first in a series of films about artists.

«Я фотограф. Вот, пожалуй, и всё»

“I'm a photographer. That's about it.”

Malika Mokadem

How it was filmed

  1. MarseilleWe met at her exhibition in Moscow. I started filming on my own, on instinct, while staying with her in Marseille.
  2. Venice · ParisThen came meetings and travels together.
  3. MoscowThe last shoot: several days, a long interview and art scenes.
  4. NowI took time to learn AI and music. Now I'm finishing the film myself: the edit, AI scenes of her inner world, my own music.
I

Marseille

Home

A huge old apartment where every inch is an art object. This is where I began to film.

«Всё, что есть в моём доме, привезено из путешествий, это всё воспоминания»

“So everything that's in my house comes from a travel, a memory”

Malika Mokadem
II

Marseille

Behind the camera

Malika at work: she shoots, I film her.

«Всё дело в том, чтобы делиться моментами»

“It's all about sharing moments”

Malika Mokadem

A party and the morning after.

III

Marseille · the rocks

The Girl Who Could Fly

«Когда я была маленькой, я умела летать»

“I could fly when I was a little girl.”

Malika Mokadem
IV

Venice

Venice

Canals, bridges, church doors, a carnival costume. Venice with Malika is a film of its own.

«Мне нравится, что невозможно понять, в какое время сделан снимок»

“I like the fact that there's no way to know what time period the picture was taken at”

Malika Mokadem

«Это место вне времени, и у него самого время на исходе»

“It's a place that's out of time and that is running out of time, too”

Malika Mokadem
V

Six Malikas

In front of the camera she turns from one self into another, as if there really were six.

«Это просто шесть человек, которым нужно найти способ существовать в одном маленьком теле»

“That's just six people that need to find a way to exist inside just this one little tiny body”

Malika Mokadem
VI

Moscow

The Last Shoot

Several days in Moscow: a long interview and art scenes. A snake, a dove, a crown of thorns.

«Я смешиваю в своей работе много разной символики»

“I mix a lot of different symbolism in my work”

Malika Mokadem

«Искусство должно быть только про эмоции, и жизнь тоже»

“Art should only be about emotion and life should only be about emotion”

Malika Mokadem

What comes next

The beginning of a series of films

MALIKA opens a series of films about the inner world of an artist and its symbolism. I want to make such films about other remarkable artists too.

The film is in post-production: black and white, in English.

Partners, patrons and foundations who feel close to this story are welcome to join the film and the series.

About Malika

Malika Mokadem is a French photographer, born in La Ciotat in 1973. She lived in San Francisco for about ten years and returned to France in 2003. She started as a model, stylist and photographer's assistant. She shoots only in daylight and barely retouches. She has illustrated books of poets and is now making her own book of photography, art and poetry.
