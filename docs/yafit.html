YAFIT by Perel

One campaign. A whole season.

YAFIT by Perel had only catalogue shots on white. I gave the brand a campaign: two heroines, the real garments and dozens of Mediterranean locations.

Client
YAFIT by Perel, Israel
Scope
Casting, locations, AI campaign art direction
Output
Frames for site, ads and social

Two faces of the brand

I cast two heroines: a Botticelli redhead and a dark Mediterranean beauty. They carry the whole campaign, so the brand becomes recognisable.

02

White doesn't sell

A catalogue shows the garment. I wanted people to want to wear it. I rebuild every piece from the collection references: print, stripe, bow, embroidery.

Stone and blue

Walls of colour

Yellow and pink. I set the garment against pure colour, and it works as a banner without a word.

City and details

Arches and stairs

For the quieter pieces I chose architecture: arches, spiral stairs, checkered floors. Another city, the same brand.

07

What the brand gets

Frames for every channel: site banners, full-length shots, pairs, details, accessories and faceless crops for product cards.

How this was made

I direct, select and finish every frame myself. The images are generated with AI tools and checked frame by frame: light, fabric, hands and product accuracy. Every commercial project comes with a written note on AI use and a usage licence. The models are composite characters. The garments are reproduced from the collection references.
